Kisan Vikas Patra Scheme
Level: National
Category: Small Savings & Investment Certificate
Key Benefit: Investment doubles in 115 months (9 years & 7 months) at 7.5% p.a. compounded interest, with no maximum deposit limit

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* Any individual who is a resident of India can avail the benefits under the scheme.
* An adult can purchase a certificate for himself/herself, or on behalf of a minor, or on behalf of a person of unsound mind.
* Minors aged 10 years and above can open/purchase an account in their own name.
* Joint Accounts: Up to 3 adults can hold a joint account together.

Account Types

* Single Holder Type Account: Opened by an adult, on behalf of a minor/person of unsound mind, or by a minor aged 10+ years.
* Joint A-Type Account: Payable to all account holders jointly or to the survivor(s) (up to 3 adults).
* Joint B-Type Account: Payable to any of the account holders or to the survivor(s) (up to 3 adults).

Deposit Limits & Maturity

* Minimum Deposit: ₹1,000 (and in multiples of ₹100).
* Maximum Deposit: No maximum limit on deposits or number of accounts held.
* Maturity & Doubling Period: Doubles in 115 months (9 years & 7 months) at 7.5% compounded annual interest (maturity tenor determined by interest rate active at account opening).

===================================================
PREMATURE CLOSURE, TRANSFER & RULES
===================================================

Premature Encashment / Closure

* Standard Premature Lock-in: Can be encashed anytime after 2 years and 6 months (2.5 years) from the date of purchase at specified rates.
* Special Premature Closure (Before 2.5 Years): Permitted on the death of account holder(s), court orders, or forfeiture by a Gazetted Officer pledgee (earns Post Office Savings Account interest rate).

Account & Certificate Transfer

* Can be transferred from one individual to another (provided transferee is eligible) on holder's death to legal heirs/nominees, court orders, pledging, or to surviving joint holders.
* Can be transferred from one Post Office/bank branch to another.

Death of Account Holder

* Nominee/legal heirs (if 3 or fewer) may choose to continue the account until maturity or close it immediately.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Offline)

* Step 01: Visit nearest Post Office branch or designated bank (in person or via authorized small savings agent).
* Step 02: Collect or download the application form, fill out details, declaration, and nomination info.
* Step 03: Submit form with required documents and initial deposit amount.
* Step 04: Payment modes include Cash, locally executed Cheque/Demand Draft, or Post Office Savings account withdrawal form.
* Step 05: Certificate/Receipt issued upon realization/payment. Keep document safe for maturity encashment.

Documents Required

* Passport size Photo.
* Identity & Address Proof (Officially Valid Documents):
   * Aadhaar Card / Passport / Driving License / Voter ID Card / NREGA Job Card / National Population Register Letter.
* Proof of Age (Birth Certificate for minors).
* Copy of PAN Card.
