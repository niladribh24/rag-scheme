Coffee Development Programme in North Eastern Region: Expansion of Coffee
Level: Regional (North Eastern Region)
Category: Agriculture / DBT / Farmer Subsidy
Key Benefit: 50% subsidy on unit cost (₹50,000 per hectare out of ₹1,00,000 unit cost) released in two installments via direct bank transfer

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* The applicant must be a tribal grower.
* The applicant's land area must be suitable for coffee cultivation as per the opinion of Coffee Board officers.
* The applicant must procure seed material exclusively from the Coffee Board.

===================================================
BENEFITS & SUBSIDY RELEASE STRUCTURE
===================================================

Financial Assistance & Installments

* Unit Cost: ₹1,00,00,00 / ₹1,00,000 per hectare.
* Scale of Subsidy: 50% of the Unit Cost (i.e., ₹50,000 per hectare).
* Release Mechanism:
   * 1st Installment: 60% of the eligible subsidy amount (released after completion of planting).
   * 2nd Installment: 40% of the eligible subsidy amount (released during the 2nd year of planting after vacancy filling).
* Payment Mode: Direct transfer to the grower's bank account via EFT / RTGS / NEFT by Joint Director (Extn.) / Deputy Director (Extn.).

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Offline)

Claiming 1st Installment:
* Step 01: Print the prescribed application form.
* Step 02: Fill in mandatory details, paste passport-sized photograph, and attach self-attested supporting documents.
* Step 03: Submit the completed application form to the concerned Extension Office of the Coffee Board after completing planting.
* Step 04: The Senior Liaison Officer (SLO) / Junior Liaison Officer (JLO) scrutinizes documents, conducts field inspection, and forwards recommendation for sanction/release.

Claiming 2nd Installment:
* Step 01: Print the prescribed application form for 2nd installment.
* Step 02: Fill required fields, paste photo, and attach mandatory enclosures.
* Step 03: Submit to the Extension Office during the 2nd year of planting after filling vacancies.
* Step 04: SLO/JLO inspects field and submits inspection report for 2nd installment release.

Documents Required

For 1st Installment Claim:
* Application and Self-Declaration in duplicate (signed by applicant).
* Proof of Photo Identity (Driving License / Ration Card / Voter ID (EPIC) / Govt-issued Identity Document / Passport / PAN Card).
* Proof of Land Ownership / Land records / Possession certificate (duly certified by Village / State / Competent Authority in the beneficiary's name).
* Bank Account details (Account Number, Bank Name, Branch, and IFSC Code).

For 2nd Installment Claim:
* Application and Self-Declaration in duplicate (signed by applicant) in the prescribed format.
