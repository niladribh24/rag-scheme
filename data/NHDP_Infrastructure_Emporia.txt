National Handicrafts Development Programme: Infrastructure And Technology Support: EMPORIA
Level: National
Category: Infrastructure & Technology Support / Handicrafts & Handloom Emporia
Key Benefit: Financial assistance up to ₹60 Lakh for new emporia and ₹15 Lakh for strengthening/renovation (80%-100% government funding)

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Implementing Agencies

* Central / State Handloom and Handicrafts Development Corporations and other eligible Govt. Corporations / agencies promoted by State / Local Govt. bodies.
* Eligible Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs), Registered Self Help Groups (SHGs), local statutory bodies, exporter bodies/associations, Apex Cooperative Societies, and National level Apex Societies (registered under Societies Act / Trust Act).
* Organizations such as IICT, MHSC, HMCM, NIFT, and Export Promotion Councils.
* Producer Companies registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act working for the promotion and development of Handicrafts & Handloom.
* Direct departmental implementation by O/o DC (Handicrafts).

===================================================
FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE & FUNDING PATTERN
===================================================

Component-Wise Cost Ceiling & Grants

* New Emporia (Rented / Own Building): Per unit cost ceiling of ₹60,00,000.
   * General States: 80% borne by O/o DC (Handicrafts); 20% contributed by Implementing Agency (IA).
   * Special Regions (NER, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep): 90% borne by O/o DC (Handicrafts); 10% contributed by IA.
* Strengthening / Renovation of Existing Emporia: Per unit cost ceiling of ₹15,00,000.
   * 100% assistance borne by Office of the DC (Handicrafts).
* Building Rental: Entire rental amount (if applicable) must be borne by the Implementing Agency.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Offline)

* Step 01: Prepare proposals in the prescribed proforma along with all mandatory self-attested supporting documents.
* Step 02: Submit hard copies of the completed proposal to the concerned Handicraft Service Center (HSC), Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts) within the specified timeline.
* Step 03: Proposals are reviewed and recommended by Field/Regional offices before approval and execution of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU).

Documents Required

For Fresh Proposals:
* Proposal in prescribed proforma with Recommendation Letter from Field/Regional Office.
* Valid Registration Certificate, Article & Memorandum of Association, and Bye-Laws.
* 3 years Balance Sheet & audited statement of accounts, plus Annual Report of the last year.
* Affidavit regarding Non-Corrupt Practice and validity of Registration.
* Land ownership document / Lease agreement / Detailed Project Report (DPR).
* Feasibility report certified by a Scheduled Commercial Bank.
* Construction plan and cost estimate authenticated by an Architect / Chartered Engineer.
* Third-party assessment report from a recognized Chartered Engineer / technical valuer for equipment/machines.
* Undertaking for bearing the balance cost / escalated cost.

For Reimbursement Proposals:
* Monthly Performance / Achievement Report with photographs.
* Verification Report (Gist of expenditure) with mode of payment from Assistant Director, HSC/Field formation.
* Utilization Certificate in GFR 12A verified by Chartered Accountant (with Membership No.) and countersigned by IA.
* Audited Statement of Accounts & Expenditure verified by CA and countersigned by IA.
* Inspection Report from concerned Assistant Director of the field office.

Mandatory Certifications (For All Proposals):
1. No financial assistance received from other sources for the same purpose.
2. Acceptance of scheme provisions and commitment to project completion within timeline.
3. Availability of required infrastructure.
4. No duplication of efforts with other Ministry schemes.
5. Organization not involved in corrupt practices nor blacklisted by central/state agencies.
6. No Utilization Certificate (UC) pending under any scheme of O/o DC (Handicrafts).
