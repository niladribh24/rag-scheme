National Food Security Mission (NFSM)
Level: National
Category: Agriculture / DBT / Demonstration & Seed Distribution
Key Benefit: Production enhancement via cluster demonstrations (up to ₹15,000/ha), DBT seed distribution, nutrient/pest management, and farm machinery support

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants & Beneficiary Target Allocations

* All farmers are eligible to apply.
* Land Ceiling: Beneficiaries are entitled to avail assistance across various components up to a maximum of 5 hectares in a single season.
* Dedicated Allocations:
   * Scheduled Castes (SCP): 16% of total allocation (or proportionate to SC population in State/District).
   * Scheduled Tribes (TSP): 8% of total allocation (or proportionate to ST population in State/District).
   * Small & Marginal Farmers: At least 33% of fund allocation.
   * Women Farmers: At least 30% of fund allocation.

Criteria for Selection of Districts & Cluster Sizes

* NFSM-Rice & NFSM-Wheat: Districts with area > 50,000 ha and yield below State average.
* Hill States (HP, J&K, Uttarakhand): Districts with at least 15,000 ha area under rice/wheat.
* North-Eastern States (excl. Assam): Districts with at least 5,000 ha area under rice.
* NFSM-Pulses: All districts across 29 states covered.
* Coarse Cereals (Maize/Barley): Districts covering 70% of total coarse cereal area in the State.
* NFSM-Nutri-Cereals (Jowar, Bajra, Ragi, Small Millets): All growing states covered.
* Minimum Cluster Sizes:
   * State Govt Demonstration: Minimum 100 ha cluster (25 ha for Hilly and North-Eastern States).
   * ICAR Cluster Front Line Demonstration (CFLD): Minimum 25 ha cluster.
   * ICAR Front Line Demonstration (FLD): Minimum 1 ha cluster.

===================================================
BENEFITS & SCHEME COMPONENTS
===================================================

Core Benefits & Financial Incentives

1. Demonstration Incentives (per hectare):
   * Sole Crop (excluding coarse cereals): ₹9,000 / ha.
   * Cropping-System Based Demonstration: ₹15,000 / ha.
   * Coarse Cereals Demonstration: ₹6,000 / ha.
2. Seed Distribution Assistance: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) assistance for certified seed purchase available up to a maximum area of 2 ha per farmer in a crop season (rates vary by crop).
3. Additional Support Components: Integrated nutrient & pest management, water carrying pipes, capacity building, and post-harvest technology flexi-components (e.g., Dal Mill, de-stoning, grading, farm machinery).

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Offline Implementation)

* Step 01: Identification and selection of beneficiary farmers in a transparent, time-bound manner with active involvement of Gram Panchayats and Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs).
* Step 02: Release of funds from Central Government to State Governments upon NFSM Executive Committee approval.
* Step 03: Fund transfer from State Government to State Level Agency via PFMS / Treasury.
* Step 04: Allocation of funds from State Level Agency to District Level Agency based on approved district programs.
* Step 05: Fund release from District Level Agency directly to Implementing Agencies or Beneficiaries.
