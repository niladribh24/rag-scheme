National Scheme of Welfare of Fishermen
Level: National
Category: Fisheries / Social Welfare / Financial Assistance
Key Benefit: Housing grants (up to ₹75,000), community hall & drinking water infrastructure, group accident insurance (up to ₹50,000 cover), and Savings-cum-Relief support.

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Inland Fishermen

* Must be officially licensed by the respective State or Union Territory.
* Age limit: Below 60 years old.
* Must belong to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.
* Must be engaged in full-time inland fishing activities.

For Marine Fishermen

* Must operate under State, Union Territory, or FISHCOPFED.
* Must be officially licensed by respective State / Union Territory authorities.
* Must be engaged full-time in fishing activities at sea.
* Must be a member of a Welfare Society, Federation, or Cooperative Society.
* Note: Fishermen enrolled via FISHCOPFED are eligible specifically for the Insurance component.

===================================================
BENEFITS & SCHEME COMPONENTS
===================================================

1. Housing Facility
* Construction of individual houses for fishermen.
* Base area: Within 35 sq. meters.
* Maximum cost ceiling: Up to ₹75,000 per house. No village-wise upper limit on the number of homes.

2. Construction of Common Facility (Community Hall)
* Applicable in villages with more than 75 houses.
* Infrastructure: Community hall with two toilets and a tube well.
* Base area: 200 sq. meters.
* Maximum cost ceiling: Up to ₹2,00,000.
* Usage: Serves as a community hall, mending shed, and drying yard.

3. Provision of Clean Drinking Water
* 1 tube well installed for every 20 houses (scalable based on requirement).
* Alternative drinking water arrangements provided where tube well installation is unfeasible.

4. Group Accident Insurance
* Coverage:
   * Death or Permanent Total Disability: ₹50,000 cover.
   * Partial Permanent Disability: ₹25,000 cover.
* Policy & Premium: 12-month coverage policy managed by FISHCOPFED.
   * Total annual premium: ₹15 per head.
   * Funding ratio: 50% Central Govt subsidy and 50% State Govt subsidy (100% Central funding for Union Territories). Central share is released directly through FISHCOPFED for participating States/UTs.

5. Savings-cum-Relief Scheme
* Contribution: Marine fishermen contribute ₹75 per month for 8 months (Total ₹600).
* Government Matching Contribution: State and Central Governments contribute a matching amount of ₹600 (50:50 sharing basis).
* Default Rule: If a fisherman fails to complete payment, contributions are refunded with interest at the end of the 4th month.
* Lean Month Distribution: Period determined by FISHCOPFED depending on coastal/marine region rules.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Offline)

* Step 01: Eligible fishermen visit the nearest FISHCOPFED office / Fisheries Department office to submit the application.
* Step 02: President or Secretary of the Fishermen Association collects contributions and deposits them into the designated nationalized bank account chosen by the Director of Fisheries.
* Step 03: Central and State Governments deposit their matching financial contributions.
* Step 04: Upon maturity/lean period, accumulated funds along with total accrued interest are disbursed to the beneficiary.

Documents Required

* Application form in the prescribed format.
* Passport-sized photograph of the applicant (with spouse, if married).
* Fishing Vessel Registration Certificate (issued by the Directorate of Fisheries).
* Current Net License payment receipt.
* Professional-cum-Residential Certificate.
* Copy of Ration Card.
* Income Certificate (establishing BPL / eligibility status).
* General Identity/Proof documents.
