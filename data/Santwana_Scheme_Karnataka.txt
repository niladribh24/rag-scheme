Santwana Scheme - Karnataka
Level: State (Karnataka)
Category: Minority Community Relief & Rehabilitation Loan/Subsidy
Key Benefit: Up to ₹5,00,000 unit cost (50% loan + 50% subsidy)

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* The applicant should belong to the religious minority category of Karnataka state.
* The applicant should be a permanent resident of Karnataka.
* The applicant's age should be between 18 to 55 years.
* The total family income from all sources should not exceed ₹8,00,000/-.
* The applicant's family members should not be employees in the Central or State Government.
* The applicant or his/her family should not have availed a loan facility from Karnataka Minorities Development Corporation (except Arivu Education Loan Scheme) in the last five years.
* Target Beneficiaries: Religious minorities whose houses/shops have been destroyed due to natural calamities or communal violence.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Online)

* Step 01: Visit the official portal (https://kmdconline.karnataka.gov.in/Portal/home).
* Step 02: Click "Apply Online" and enter Mobile Number to submit.
* Step 03: Enter Aadhaar Number, fill captcha, and click "Next".
* Step 04: Enter OTP received on the Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
* Step 05: Allow the official notice and re-verify OTP.
* Step 06: Select scheme name, fill basic details (father's name, gender, qualifications), and address details.
* Step 07: Upload required documents and click "Proceed".
* Step 08: Click "Submit Application" to generate an Application ID.

Documents Required

* Photo of the Applicant.
* Caste/Minority Certificate (issued by competent authority).
* Income Certificate (issued by competent authority).
* Copy of Aadhaar as Residential Proof.
* Copy of Bank Passbook.
* Self-declaration form from Surety.
* Applicant's Self-Declaration.
* Report from the relevant competent authority for aggrieved beneficiaries.
