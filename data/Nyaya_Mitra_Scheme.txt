Nyaya Mitra Scheme
Level: National
Category: Judiciary & Legal Assistance
Key Benefit: Expeditious disposal of 10-15 year old pending cases in High Courts and Subordinate Courts

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Nyaya Mitras

* The applicant must be a retired judicial officer or a retired executive officer.
* The applicant must possess legal experience or an educational background in law.

For Beneficiaries (Litigants)

* The applicant must be a litigant whose case has been languishing in the subordinate courts for more than ten years.
* The applicant must be an individual for whom quality legal aid is not available due to social and economic barriers.
* The applicant must be suffering due to a delay in investigations or trial.

Other Key Details & Place of Work

* Scheme Implementation: Department of Justice, Ministry of Law and Justice (launched April 2017).
* Key Functions: Active identification of delayed cases via NJDG, legal advice, connecting litigants to DLSA/CSC Tele Law, referral to Lok Adalats, assisting prison reforms, and analysis of pending bottlenecks.
* Place of Work (High Court): Allocation of sitting space will be made by the respective High Court.
* Place of Work (District Level): Sitting space provided either at DLSA or District Court (no clerical support provided).

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Offline)

* Step 01: Visit the designated District Facilitation Centres housed within the Common Service Centers of selected districts to seek legal assistance. The Nyaya Mitra actively identifies pending cases using the National Judicial Data Grid and reaches out to affected parties.
* Step 02: Interact with the Nyaya Mitra for a critical analysis of the pending case to understand specific bottlenecks causing judicial delay, receive legal advice, and explore practical solutions.
* Step 03: Follow the Nyaya Mitra's guidance to connect with the District Legal Services Authority, Common Service Centers Tele Law, or civil society organizations. Suitable cases are systematically referred to Lok Adalats for prioritized dispute resolution.

Documents Required

* Proof of Retirement as a Judicial Officer or Executive Officer (for Nyaya Mitras).
* Proof of Legal Experience or Legal Background (for Nyaya Mitras).
* Pending Case Details and Case History from the National Judicial Data Grid.
* Proof of Socio-Economic Status or Income Certificate.
* Valid Identity Proof of the Applicant.
