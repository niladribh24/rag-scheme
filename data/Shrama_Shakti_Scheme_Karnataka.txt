Shrama Shakti Scheme - Karnataka
Level: State (Karnataka)
Category: Religious Minority Community Entrepreneurship & Skill Loan / Subsidy
Key Benefit: Loan of ₹50,000 at 4% interest with 50% backend subsidy upon timely repayment

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* The age limit of the applicant should be between 18 and 55 years.
* The applicant must be a permanent resident of the State (Karnataka).
* Applicants must belong to the State Religious Minority Community.
* Family income from all sources should be within ₹3.50 lakh per annum.
* An applicant or his/her family members should not have availed benefits under any other scheme (excluding the Arivu Scheme) of the KMDCL in the last 5 years.

Other Key Details & Loan Rules

* Loan Details: Loan of ₹50,000 at 4% interest rate, to be paid in 36 installments.
* Training: Entrepreneurs will be trained in artistic and technical skills.
* Backend Subsidy Rule: If 50% of the loan is repaid within 36 months, the remaining 50% is treated as a backend subsidy.
* Default Condition: If the loan is not repaid within 36 months, 50% of the backend subsidy will be treated as a loan.
* Selection Panel Structure:
   * Chairman: Concerned Deputy Commissioner of the District
   * Vice-Chairman: Chief Executive Officer of the Zilla Panchayat
   * Member: Lead Bank Manager of the concerned District
   * Member: Concerned District Regional Transport Officer
   * Member: District Officer of the concerned District, Department of Minority Welfare
   * Member Secretary: District Manager of KMDC in the concerned district

===================================================
EXCLUSIONS
===================================================

* No member of the applicant's family should be an employee of State/Central/Government Public Sector Units.
* The applicant shall not be a loan defaulter in KMDC.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Online & Offline)

* Step 01: Go to the official website and fill out the online application form.
* Step 02: Print the completed application form.
* Step 03: Submit the printed application form along with relevant documents to the Selection Panel of your concerned district.
* Step 04: Upon approval by the Selection Panel, the subsidy/loan will be processed and transferred to your bank account.

Documents Required

* Printout of the completely filled online application form.
* Beneficiary's latest 2 passport-size photos.
* Caste and Income certificate.
* Copy of the Identity Proof.
* Project report of the business.
* Copy of the Bank Passbook.
* Self-Declaration Form.
* Self-Declaration Form from Surety.
