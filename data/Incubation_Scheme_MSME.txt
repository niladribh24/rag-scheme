Incubation Scheme (MSME)
Level: National
Category: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises / Business & Entrepreneurship Incubation
Key Benefit: Up to ₹15,00,000 per idea for developing and nurturing ideas, plus up to ₹1,00,00,000 capital support for plant and machinery to HI

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Host Institutes (HI) / Business Incubators (BI)

* Eligible Institutions:
   * Technical Colleges and Universities.
   * Professional Colleges / Institutes.
   * R&D Institutes.
   * NGOs involved in incubation activities.
   * MSME Development Institutes (MSME-DIs) / Technology Centres.
   * Any Institute or Organization of Central or State Government.

Key Scheme Activities

* Recognition of eligible institutions as Host Institute (HI) to act as Business Incubator (BI).
* Approval of ideas of incubatees submitted through Host Institute (HI).
* Assistance for nurturing of ideas to HI.
* Assistance towards capital support to HI for plant and machinery.

===================================================
BENEFITS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
===================================================

* Idea Nurturing Grant: Up to a maximum of ₹15,00,000 per idea provided to HI for developing and nurturing the ideas.
* Capital Support: Up to ₹1,00,00,000 (maximum) provided to HI for procurement and installation of relevant plant and machines (including hardware, software, etc.) in BI for R&D activities and common facilities for incubatees.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Online)

Registration:
* Step 01: Visit the official website and click on the "HI REGISTRATION" button.
* Step 02: Enter all required institutional details and click "Submit".
* Step 03: Upon successful registration, a confirmation message will be displayed; click "OK" to proceed to the login window.

HI/BI Application for Approval:
* Step 01: Log in to access the HI/BI dashboard, click on "Apply for HI/BI Approval", and then click "Apply".
* Step 02: Complete all institutional details in the application window and click "Final Submit".

Documents Required

* University Affiliation document.
* AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) Approval certificate.
* NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories) or other relevant accreditation.
* Audited annual accounts with auditor’s report for the last two years.
* Any other supporting documents as required by the competent authority.
