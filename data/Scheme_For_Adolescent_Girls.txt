Scheme For Adolescent Girls
Level: National
Category: Women & Child Development / Empowerment & Health / Nutrition & Life Skills
Key Benefit: Fortified Take Home Ration (600 kcal, 18-20g protein/day for 300 days/year), IFA supplementation, health check-ups, and life skills/vocational training

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* Target Beneficiaries: Adolescent girls aged 14 to 18 years.
* Identification: Must be identified by the concerned State Government.
* Geographical Coverage: Must reside in areas covered under the scheme, including Aspirational Districts across States and North-Eastern States (including Assam).
* Identification Requirement: Possession of an Aadhaar number is required to avail scheme benefits.

===================================================
BENEFITS & SCHEME COMPONENTS
===================================================

1. Nutritional Benefits
* Supplementary Nutrition provided in the form of Take Home Ration (THR).
* Nutrient Standard: Contains 600 calories, 18–20 grams of protein, and fortified micronutrients per day.
* Duration: Provided for 300 days in a year.
* Quality & Tracking: Age-specific processed food mixtures are labeled for targeted consumption; service delivery is tracked via Poshan Tracker.

2. Non-Nutritional Benefits
* Iron and Folic Acid (IFA) supplementation.
* Health Check-up and Referral Services.
* Nutrition & Health Education (NHE).
* Promotion of menstrual hygiene awareness.
* Peer Educator Programme encouraging participation to address adolescent issues.
* Skill training and vocational development to foster livelihood opportunities.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Offline)

* Step 01: The applicant approaches the nearest Anganwadi Centre (AWC).
* Step 02: State Government identifies and verifies eligible adolescent girls under the scheme.
* Step 03: Beneficiary details are recorded and submitted by the State to the Ministry.
* Step 04: The applicant's Aadhaar number is registered to facilitate service delivery.
* Step 05: Post-registration, the beneficiary receives Take Home Ration (THR) and non-nutritional services via designated delivery platforms.

Documents Required

* Aadhaar Card / Aadhaar details [cite: 31, 32]
* Proof of Age / Date of Birth (DOB) Certificate [cite: 32]
