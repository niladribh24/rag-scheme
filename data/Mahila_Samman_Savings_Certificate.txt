Mahila Samman Savings Certificate
Level: National
Category: Investment & Small Savings for Women and Girl Children
Key Benefit: Fixed interest of 7.5% p.a. compounded quarterly, with deposits up to ₹2,00,000 for a 2-year tenure

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* The applicant must possess Indian citizenship.
* The scheme is exclusively for women and girl children.
* Any individual woman can apply for herself, or a guardian can open an account on behalf of a minor girl.
* There is no upper age limit; women of all ages can avail the benefits.
* Account Structure: Single-holder type account. An individual may open multiple accounts subject to the maximum aggregate ceiling of ₹2,00,000, maintaining a minimum time gap of 3 months between account openings.

Deposit Limits & Terms

* Minimum Deposit: ₹1,000 (and in multiples of ₹100).
* Maximum Deposit: Ceiling of ₹2,00,000 across all accounts held by an account holder.
* Tenure & Maturity: Duration of 2 years from the date of deposit. Maturity value rounded off to the nearest rupee.
* Partial Withdrawal: Up to 40% of the eligible balance can be withdrawn once after completion of 1 year from the opening date.

===================================================
PREMATURE CLOSURE & RULES
===================================================

* Extreme Compassionate Grounds (Full Interest): Account can be closed before maturity on death of account holder, or medical support for life-threatening diseases of account holder, or death of guardian (where continuation causes undue hardship). Interest paid at standard 7.5% scheme rate.
* Other Reasons (Interest Penalty): Premature closure permitted anytime after 6 months for any other reason, but interest will be calculated at 2% less than the scheme rate (i.e., 5.5%).
* Irregular Accounts: Any deposit not in consonance with scheme rules earns interest at the standard Post Office Savings Account rate.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Offline)

* Step 01: Visit nearest Post Office branch or designated bank to collect application form (or download online).
* Step 02: Fill out application form, attach required documents, declaration, and nomination details.
* Step 03: Submit form along with initial deposit amount (cash or cheque) via pay-in-slip.
* Step 04: Receive official certificate serving as proof of investment.

Documents Required

* Passport size photograph.
* Proof of Age (Birth Certificate).
* Identity & Address Proof:
   * Identity Proof (e.g. Identity Card / Passport / Driving License / Voter ID / NREGA Job Card / National Population Register Letter).
   * PAN Card.
* Pay-in-Slip with deposit amount or cheque.
