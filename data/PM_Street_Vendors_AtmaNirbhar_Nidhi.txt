PM Street Vendor’s AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi)
Level: National
Category: Housing & Urban Affairs / Credit & Working Capital Support for Street Vendors
Key Benefit: Collateral-free working capital loans in tranches up to ₹50,000, 7% interest subsidy, and digital cashback up to ₹100/month

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* Street vendors in possession of Certificate of Vending / Identity Card issued by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs).
* Street vendors identified in the survey but not yet issued Certificate of Vending / Identity Card.
* Street vendors left out of the ULB identification survey or who started vending after the survey, having a Letter of Recommendation (LoR) issued by ULB / Town Vending Committee (TVC).
* Vendors from surrounding peri-urban / rural areas vending within ULB geographical limits with an LoR from ULB / TVC.

===================================================
BENEFITS & LOAN STRUCTURE
===================================================

Working Capital Loan Tranches

* 1st Tranche: Loan up to ₹15,000 (repayment period up to 12 months).
* 2nd Tranche: Loan up to ₹25,000 (repayment period up to 18 months).
* 3rd Tranche: Loan up to ₹50,000 (repayment period up to 36 months).
* Early/Timely Repayment: Unlocks higher loan tranches without prepayment penalty.

Interest Subsidy & Additional Benefits

* Interest Subsidy: 7% per annum credited quarterly directly to the beneficiary's bank account (valid for standard/non-NPA accounts up to 31st March 2033).
* Digital Transaction Cashback: Up to ₹100 per month for conducting digital transactions.
* UPI-Linked RuPay Credit Card: Initial credit limit of ₹10,000 (extendable up to ₹30,000; valid for 5 years), available after successful repayment of 2nd tranche loan.
* Credit Guarantee Cover: 31.87% cover for 1st tranche, 8.25% for 2nd tranche, and 6% for 3rd tranche (no charges for lending institutions).

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Online / Offline)

* Step 01: Visit the official portal (https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/) and click "Log In".
* Step 02: Enter mobile number and OTP to log in.
* Step 03: Select vendor category and enter mandatory Survey Reference Number (SRN) or LoR details.
* Step 04: Fill out the online application form, upload required documents, and submit.

Documents Required

* For 1st Loan:
   * Category A & B: Certificate of Vending (CoV) / Identity Card.
   * Category C & D: Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from ULB/TVC.
* KYC Documents (in addition to CoV/ID/LoR):
   * Aadhaar details / Voter ID Card / Driving License / MNREGA Card / PAN Card.
* Documents for Letter of Recommendation (LoR) Request (if applicable):
   * Account Statement / Passbook copy.
   * Membership card or proof of membership in vendor associations.
   * Document substantiating claim as a vendor and formal request letter to ULB.
* For 2nd / Subsequent Loans:
   * Previous Loan Closure Document.
