National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme-2 (NAPS-2)
Level: National
Category: Skill Development & Apprenticeship Support
Key Benefit: Partial stipend support up to ₹1,500/month, On-the-job experiential training

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Apprentices

* Minimum age for an apprentice is 14 years, and 18 years for designated trades related to hazardous industries, as per Section 3(a) of the Apprentices Act, 1961.
* To avail partial stipend support by the Government, at the time of registration the upper age limit should be 35 years.
* Candidates from skill development programmes such as Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDUGKY), National Urban Livelihoods Mission (NULM), Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY), etc. will be encouraged to take up apprenticeship training.
* Candidates must have updated their Aadhaar numbers and completed e-KYC on the apprenticeship portal to be issued a contract by establishments.

Other Key Details & Stipend Rules

* Stipend Support: Partial stipend support by GoI under NAPS-2 is limited to 25% of stipend paid, up to a maximum of ₹ 1,500/- per month, per apprentice through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
* Minimum Rate of Stipend Payable to Apprentices per Month:
   * School pass-outs (Class 5th – Class 9th): ₹ 5,000/-
   * School pass-outs (Class 10th): ₹ 6,000/-
   * School pass-outs (Class 12th): ₹ 7,000/-
   * National or State Certificate holder: ₹ 7,000/-
   * Technician (vocational) apprentice or Vocational Certificate holder or Sandwich Course (Diploma Students): ₹ 7,000/-
   * Technician apprentices or Diploma holder in any stream or Sandwich Course (Degree Students): ₹ 8,000/-
   * Graduate apprentices or Degree apprentices or Degree in any stream: ₹ 9,000/-

===================================================
EXCLUSIONS
===================================================

* No reimbursement of basic training cost will be provided under NAPS-2, both for Designated Trades (DT) and Optional Trades (OT).
* No stipend support will be available under NAPS-2 to Central and State Government Departments and Central and State Public Sector Undertakings/Enterprises, including Public Sector Banks.
