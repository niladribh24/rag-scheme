Financial Assistance as Interest Subsidy on Home Loan
Level: National
Category: Housing Loan Interest Subsidy for Defense Personnel
Key Benefit: 50% interest reimbursement on home loan up to ₹1,00,000 for max 5 years

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* The applicant must be a war widow, war bereaved family member, war disabled personnel, or attributable peacetime casualty (all ranks).
* The applicant must have taken a home loan from a bank, government/public sector undertaking, LIC, GIC, or HUDCO.
* The loan amount considered for subsidy must not exceed ₹1,00,000, regardless of the actual loan amount.

Other Key Details & Loan Rules

* Interest Subsidy Amount: 50% of the interest charged on the home loan.
* Loan Limit for Subsidy: Up to ₹1,00,000 (even if the loan amount is higher).
* Duration: Maximum of 5 years or until loan repayment is completed, whichever is earlier.
* Applicable Loan Sources: Banks, Government/Public Sector undertakings, Life Insurance Corporation (LIC), General Insurance Corporation (GIC), and Housing and Urban Development Corporation (HUDCO).

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Online)

* Step 01: Register on the official portal (https://www.ksb.gov.in/) with name, Aadhaar card number, email ID, mobile number, and bank details.
* Step 02: Log in using the generated User ID and password.
* Step 03: Select "New Application" and choose the scheme name.
* Step 04: Fill in the application form and upload required documents (photograph, caste certificate, residential certificate, bank passbook, etc.).
* Step 05: Click "Save & Forward" and print the application form.
* Step 06: Submit the printed application along with required documents to the District Sainik Board for vetting and approval by KSB Secretariat.

Documents Required

* Certificates from concerned banker/organization (Govt/PSU/LIC/GIC/HUDCO):
   * A certificate stating no change in the repayment schedule.
   * Statement showing actual interest paid for the covered period.
   * Certificate confirming regular repayment of loan along with interest.
* Applicant's Documents:
   * Attested copy of Ex-Servicemen Identity Card.
   * Attested copy of Discharge Book.
