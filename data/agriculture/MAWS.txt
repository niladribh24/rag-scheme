Meghalaya Agriculture Warehouse Scheme
Level: State (Meghalaya)
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment
Key Benefit: The scheme reduces post-harvest losses and supports farmers by providing loans and subsidies to build small, weatherproof warehouses for safe storage and better marketing of crops.

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

**For Individuals:**

  1. The applicant should be between 18 and 58 years of age.
  2. The applicant should be a permanent resident of Meghalaya.
  3. The applicant should have ownership of the land where the warehouse is proposed or leased land for at least the next 10 years.
  4. The applicant should not be a credit defaulter of any bank.
**For Registered Entities:**

  1. The applicant should be based in the village where the program is being availed.
  2. The applicant should have an active bank account and submit the bank statement of the last six months.
  3. The applicant should have been in operation for at least two years.
  4. The applicant should possess land, either self-owned or leased, duly verified by the competent authority.
  5. The applicant should not be a credit defaulter of any bank.

===================================================
SCHEME DETAILS & BENEFITS
===================================================

1. 5% of project cost to be contributed by the entrepreneur 75% of project cost provided upfront by Government of Meghalaya as margin money support to the bank remaining 20% via bank loan.
  2. Direct credit linkage with partner banks.
  3. Guidance and handholding in the application process through implementing agencies.
  4. Support in project realization for the warehouse venture.
  5. Training and capacity building for entrepreneurs to manage post-harvest storage effectively.
  6. Technical support for maintenance and operations of warehouses.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process:

Visit the official government scheme portal or nearest citizen service center to apply.

Documents Required:

**Individuals**

  1. Proof of Address of Applicant (Voter ID / Passport) (If available)
  2. Ration Card of Applicant
  3. PAN Card
  4. Latest Bank Statement (Last 1 Year)
  5. Self-attested statement on land availability
  6. Self Declaration Certificate
**Registered Entities**

  1. Self-attested declaration certificate for Registered Society
  2. Ration Card of Applicant
  3. Incorporation Certificate or Registration Certificate
  4. PAN Card
  5. GST Certificate (if available)
  6. Proof of Address of Applicant (Voter ID / Passport / Driving License) - President or Secretary
  7. Latest Bank Statement (Last 1 Year)
  8. Self-declarationof President/Secretary of SHG/IVCS/FPO/Others
  9. Self-attestedstatement on land availability
