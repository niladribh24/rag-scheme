===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

* The beneficiary should be land holder.
  * The beneficiary farmer should have minimum 1 acre Agricultural land.
  * The beneficiary farmer must have his own bank account (Bank passbook and cancelled check).
  * The beneficiary must have Aadhar card as identity proof.
  * The beneficiary must have a Certified bill of purchase of equipment/items.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process:

Visit the official government scheme portal or nearest citizen service center to apply.

Documents Required:

1. Aadhar Card (Mandatory).
  2. Land Ownership Documents (minimum 1 acre) (Mandatory).
  3. Bank Passbook and Cancelled Cheque (for financial transactions) (Mandatory).
  4. Certified Purchase Bill of Equipment/Items (Mandatory).
  5. Signed Online Application Form (from i-Khedut Portal) (Mandatory).
  6. Any other document if required.
