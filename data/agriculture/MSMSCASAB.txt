===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

1. The applicant must be a student.
  2. The applicant must be enrolled in an M.Sc. in Agricultural Science or Agricultural Biotechnology program at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar.
  3. The scholarship is awarded for a duration of three semesters only.

===================================================
SCHEME DETAILS & BENEFITS
===================================================

**Rate of Scholarship:**₹1,000/- per month (For 3 semesters only).

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process:

Visit the official government scheme portal or nearest citizen service center to apply.

Documents Required:

Documents are required in accordance with the university guidelines prescribed for this scheme.
