Chief Minister's Agriculture Mechanisation Programme
Level: State (Arunachal Pradesh)
Category: Agriculture,Rural & Environment
Key Benefit: The scheme "Chief Minister's Agriculture Mechanisation Programme" was started by the government of Arunachal Pradesh to promote mechanization & hiring services for various agric...

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

1. All categories of farmers viz; Marginal, Small, Medium, and Large Farmers.
  2. All the SHGs, Co-operative Societies, FPOs, and Entrepreneurs as identified by District Nodal Agencies, DAO as Member Secretary, DHO, DAH & VO, and DFDO as members.
  3. A farmer or all members of a group of farmers should be first-time buyers of the tractor/ farm machinery.
  4. Farmers should not own a tractor/ farm machinery now or earlier in or their name or in the names of his/ their family members.
  1. Facilities for after-sale services and spare parts should reasonably be available in the area/ district where the scheme is to be implemented.
