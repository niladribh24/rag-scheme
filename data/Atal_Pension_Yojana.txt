Atal Pension Yojana
Level: National
Category: Pension & Social Security for Unorganized Workers
Key Benefit: Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 after 60 years of age

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* The applicant must be between 18 and 40 years of age.
* The applicant must possess an active Savings Bank or Post Office account.
* The subscriber must contribute from the age of joining until reaching 60 years of age via auto-debit facility (monthly, quarterly, or half-yearly).

===================================================
EXCLUSIONS
===================================================

* From 1st October 2022, any citizen who is or has been an income tax payer is not eligible to join APY.

Other Key Details & Benefit Breakdown

* Guaranteed Minimum Pension: Post 60 years, the subscriber receives a monthly pension of ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000, or ₹5,000 until death.
* Pension to Spouse: Upon the subscriber's demise, the spouse receives the exact same monthly pension amount until death.
* Return of Pension Wealth: Upon the demise of both subscriber and spouse, the entire accumulated pension corpus (till age 60) is returned to the nominee.
* Tax Benefits: Eligible for tax deductions under section 80CCD(1) of the Income Tax Act.
* Voluntary Exit (Before 60 Years): Refund of subscriber's contributions plus net actual accrued interest (minus account maintenance charges).
* Death Before 60 Years:
   * Option 1: Spouse can continue contributing to the APY account for the remaining vesting period till the original subscriber would have turned 60.
   * Option 2: Entire accumulated pension corpus till date is returned to the spouse/nominee.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Online & Offline)

Process 1 (Net Banking):
* Step 01: Log into your internet banking account and search for "Atal Pension Yojana".
* Step 02: Fill in basic personal and nominee details.
* Step 03: Provide consent for auto-debit of contribution from your savings account and submit.

Process 2 (eNPS / e-APY Portal):
* Step 01: Visit https://enps.nsdl.com/eNPS/NationalPensionSystem.html and select "Atal Pension Yojana" -> "APY Registration".
* Step 02: Fill basic details and complete KYC via Offline XML, Aadhaar OTP verification, or Virtual ID.
* Step 03: Receive acknowledgment number, fill personal details, select desired pension amount, and choose contribution frequency.
* Step 04: Provide nominee details, proceed to NSDL website for eSign, and complete registration via Aadhaar OTP verification.

Process 3 (Offline / Bank Branch):
* Step 01: Visit your nearest bank branch holding your active savings account.
* Step 02: Fill out the APY registration form, choose pension slab, and setup auto-debit instructions.

Documents Required

* Active Bank / Post Office Savings Account.
* KYC details (fetched from active Savings Account or verified via Aadhaar / Virtual ID).
* Valid Identity & Address Proof (Aadhaar / Passport / Voter ID).
* Nominee Details.
