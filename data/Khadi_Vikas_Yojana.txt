Khadi Vikas Yojana
Level: National
Category: Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises / Khadi & Village Industries
Key Benefit: Assistance including MMDA (35% subsidy on Prime Cost), ISEC (interest subsidy above 4%), and Workshed grants up to ₹1,20,000

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* The applicant must be a Khadi artisan or a Khadi Institution registered with KVIC or KVIB.
* The age of the applicant must be between 18 to 55 years.
* One person per family is eligible under the scheme.
* The applicant should not have availed benefits from any other government scheme for the same purpose.

Reservation / Preference / Priority

* Preference is given to SC/ST, women, unemployed youth, and BPL category applicants.
* Beneficiaries may be identified by KVIC, KVIBs, NGOs, DICs, FPOs, or Khadi Institutions.

Other Key Details & Component Benefits

* Modified Market Development Assistance (MMDA): Subsidy at 35% on Prime Cost for Cotton/Muslin, Wool, and Polyvastra; 20% on Prime Cost for Silk Khadi.
* Interest Subsidy Eligibility Certificate (ISEC): Khadi Institutions pay only a 4% interest rate; the difference charged by the bank is borne by KVIC as Interest Subsidy.
* Work-shed Scheme for Khadi Artisans:
   * Individual workshed (20 sq. mtrs): Up to ₹1,20,000 or 75% of cost including toilet (90% for NER), whichever is less.
   * Group workshed (10 sq. mtrs per artisan): Up to ₹80,000 per artisan or 75% of cost including toilet (90% for NER), whichever is less.
* Strengthening Infrastructure of Existing Weak Khadi Institutions: Financial assistance for revival up to ₹15.00 Lakh (Capital Expenditure + working fund).
* Marketing Infrastructure: Renovation assistance for sales outlets with a ceiling limit of ₹25.00 Lakh.
* Additional Components: Centre of Excellence for Khadi, Khadi Quality Assurance, Marketing (Exhibition), and Science & Technology (S&T).

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Online)

* Step 01: Visit the official KVIC online portal and click on “Online Registration of Khadi Institutions.”
* Step 02: Fill in all required institution details (name, address, type of registration, authorized person, number of artisans, and infrastructure info) and save the form.
* Step 03: Upon submission, receive a Username, Password, and Unique ID on registered email and mobile; download acknowledgment.
* Step 04: Log in using credentials and upload all required documents (registration certificate, list of artisans, workshed details, bylaws).
* Step 05: KVIC office verifies application and documents. Upon approval, the Certificate of Registration (CoR) is issued online for download.

Documents Required

* Government-issued Identity Proof / Aadhaar details.
* Age proof document.
* Khadi artisan card or Khadi Institution registration certificate.
* Bank account details.
* Caste certificate (if applicable).
* BPL certificate (if applicable).
* Project proposal or work-shed details (if required).
