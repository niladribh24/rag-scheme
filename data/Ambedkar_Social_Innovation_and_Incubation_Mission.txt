Ambedkar Social Innovation and Incubation Mission (ASIIM)
Level: National
Category: Social Justice / Entrepreneurship & Incubation for SC Youth
Key Benefit: Equity assistance up to ₹30,00,000 over 3 years for SC student/youth startups, with potential follow-on funding up to ₹15 Crore from VCF-SC

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* Targeted at Scheduled Caste (SC) or SC Divyang youth/students.
* Eligible Pathways:
   * Youth identified by Technology Business Incubators (TBIs), Atal Incubation Centers (AICs), STPI, or other Govt-supported incubation centers.
   * Youth identified for incubation by reputed private TBIs.
   * Students awarded under the Smart India Hackathon or Smart India Hardware Hackathon (Ministry of Education).
   * Startups nominated/supported by corporates through CSR funds.
   * Innovative ideas focusing on socio-economic development identified in TBIs (public/private).

Company Structure & Shareholding Conditions

* Selected entities must be incorporated as a Private Limited or Public Limited company before receiving funds.
* Proprietary Firms, Partnerships, OPCs, or LLPs must convert to Private Limited or Public Limited companies.
* SC entrepreneur must hold at least 51% shareholding along with management control, which must be maintained throughout the assistance period.
* Fund infusion structure: Equity, Optionally Convertible Preference Shares (OCPS), or Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS). Max conversion to equity is 49% stake after 3 years.
* Tenure of financial assistance is 10 years from first disbursement.

===================================================
EXCLUSIONS
===================================================

* Investments in negative sectors as per Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines are not eligible.

Other Key Details & Benefit Breakdown

* Financial Assistance: Up to ₹30,00,000 equity support spread over 3 years (disbursed at ₹10,00,000 annually, subject to satisfactory evaluation by TBI).
* Coverage: TBI accommodation cost, Hardware, Software, Fellowship, Travel & marketing, IP filing, Tool-room expenses, and Co-workers.
* Follow-on Venture Funding: Successful ventures qualify for funding up to ₹15,00,00,000 from the Venture Capital Fund for Scheduled Castes (VCF-SC), including hand-holding, guidance, and mentoring.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Online & Post-Application Workflow)

* Step 01: Visit official application link, click "Create an Account", fill mandatory details, and verify email/mobile via OTP.
* Step 02: Log in using credentials, navigate to the ASIIM scheme online application form, fill in required fields, upload mandatory documents, accept terms, and submit.
* Step 03 (Post-Application / Recommendation): Proposals recommended by TBIs, management/technology schools, or submitted directly by SC/SC-Divyang startups are evaluated. Selected TBI proposals receive automatic incubation selection under ASIIM.
* Step 04 (Incorporation & Compliance): Complete company conversion/incorporation to Private/Public Limited with 51% SC shareholding before fund release.

Documents Required

* Proof of SC or SC Divyang Status.
* Company Incorporation Documents (Private or Public Limited Company).
* Shareholding Pattern Document (proving minimum 51% SC shareholding & management control).
* Proof of Identification/Selection by TBIs / AICs / STPI / Private TBIs / Corporate CSR nomination.
* Proof of Award under Smart India Hackathon / Hardware Hackathon (if applicable).
* Proposal/Project Document for innovative socio-economic idea.
