Udyogini Scheme - Karnataka
Level: State (Karnataka)
Category: Women Empowerment & Self-Employment Loan / Subsidy
Key Benefit: Loan up to ₹3,00,000 with 30%-50% subsidy & EDP training for women entrepreneurs

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* The applicant must be a woman.
* The age of the applicant should be between 18 to 55 years for all categories.
* The applicant should be a permanent resident of Karnataka.
* The family income of the applicant should be less than ₹1,50,000/- per annum for general and special category women (for SC/ST women, family income limit is below ₹2,00,000 per year).
* No family income limit applies for widowed or disabled women.
* The applicant should not have defaulted on any past loan with any financial institution.

Reservation / Preference / Priority

* Preference must be given to extremely poor, destitute, widowed, and physically challenged women.
* Preference may be given to candidates who have undergone prior skill development or vocational training conducted by KSWDC or other departments.
* About 10% of the allocated target is earmarked for members of World Bank-assisted Swashakthi or Stree Shakthi groups.

Other Key Details & Benefit Breakdown

* For SC/ST Category Women: Unit cost from ₹1,00,000 up to ₹3,00,000 with a 50% subsidy.
* For Special & General Category Women: Unit cost up to ₹3,00,000 with a 30% subsidy (up to a maximum of ₹90,000/-).
* Training: Selected beneficiaries receive EDP (Entrepreneurship Development Program) training.
* Approved Activities: Micro-enterprises/trade/service sector activities such as bookbinding, notebook manufacturing, chalk/crayon manufacture, jam/jelly/pickle making, papad manufacturing, saree & embroidery work, printing/dyeing, woolen weaving, etc.
* Financing Entities: Disbursed through Commercial Banks, District Cooperative Banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), and KSFC.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Offline / Online Form Download)

* Step 01: Obtain the application form from the nearest bank, office of the Deputy Director/CDPO, or download it from www.kswdc.com.
* Step 02: Submit the completed application form along with the required documents and project proposal to the nearest bank branch or KSFC office.
* Step 03: Bank/KSFC officials verify the proposal and process the loan. The bank requests the Karnataka State Women’s Development Corporation (KSWDC) to release the subsidy amount.
* Step 04: Upon approval, the loan amount is disbursed to the applicant's bank account or directly to machinery/equipment suppliers.

Documents Required

* Three passport-size photographs of the applicant.
* Certificate regarding training/experience of the activity for which loan is sought.
* Detailed Project Report (DPR) of the business activity.
* Ration Card / Voter ID Card (Proof of identity/residence).
* Annual Income Certificate of the family.
* Caste Certificate (in case of SC/ST applicants).
* Quotations for Machinery, Equipment, and other Capital Expenses.
