Stand-Up India Scheme
Level: National
Category: SC/ST & Women Entrepreneurship Greenfield Enterprise Loan
Key Benefit: Composite loan between ₹10 Lakhs and ₹1 Crore with RuPay debit card and handholding support

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Applicants

* The applicant must be at least 18 years of age.
* Finance is provided exclusively for setting up a Greenfield Enterprise in manufacturing, services, trading, or activities allied to agriculture.
* The applicant must be a woman or belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) category. If the applicant is a male, he must belong to the SC or ST category.
* In case of non-individual enterprises, at least 51% of the shareholding and controlling stake must be held by an SC, ST, or Woman entrepreneur.
* The applicant must not be in default to any bank or financial institution.

Other Key Details & Loan Rules

* Objective: Facilitate bank loans between ₹10 Lakh and ₹1 Crore to at least one SC/ST borrower and at least one Woman borrower per bank branch.
* Loan Type: Composite loan (inclusive of term loan and working capital).
* Handholding Support: SIDBI web portal provides support through network agencies for training, skill development, mentoring, project report preparation, application filling, work shed/utility support, and subsidy schemes.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Online & Offline)

* Step 01: Visit the official portal (https://www.standupmitra.in/Login/Register), or approach the nearest bank branch, or contact the Lead District Manager (LDM) of your district.
* Step 02: Register on the portal by entering full details of the business location, selecting the applicable category (SC/ST/Woman and 51%+ stake holder), nature of proposed business, desired loan amount, and business premises details.
* Step 03: Populate fields regarding past business experience (including tenure) and indicate if handholding support is required.
* Step 04: Enter personal details (including name of enterprise and its constitution) and click "Register" to complete registration.
* Step 05: Initiate the StandUp India Loan Application process with the respective financial institution for officials to contact you and complete necessary loan formalities.

Documents Required

* Proof of Identity: Voter ID Card / Passport / Driving License / PAN Card / Signature identification from bankers of proprietor, partner, or director.
* Proof of Residence: Recent telephone bill, electricity bill, property tax receipt, Passport, or Voter ID Card of proprietor, partner, or director.
* Proof of Business Address.
* Proof that the applicant is not a defaulter in any Bank / Financial Institution.
* Memorandum and Articles of Association of the Company / Partnership Deed.
* Assets and liabilities statement of promoters and guarantors along with latest income tax returns.
* Rent Agreement (if business premises on rent) and Clearance from Pollution Control Board (if applicable).
* SSI / MSME registration (if applicable).
* Projected balance sheets for the next two years (for working capital) and for the period of the loan (for term loan).
* Photocopies of lease deeds/title deeds of all properties offered as primary and collateral securities.
* Proof of SC/ST Category status (wherever applicable).
* Certificate of Incorporation from ROC confirming 51%+ majority stake holding by SC/ST/Woman entrepreneur.
* Additional Documents for Exposure above ₹25 Lakhs:
   * Profile of the unit (promoters, directors, activities, offices/plants, shareholding pattern).
   * Last three years' balance sheets of Associate / Group Companies (if any).
   * Detailed Project Report (DPR) containing machinery details, suppliers, prices, capacity utilization, sales, projected P&L/balance sheet for loan tenor, labor details, etc.
   * Manufacturing process details, executive profiles, supplier/buyer tie-ups, competitor analysis, and strengths/weaknesses.
