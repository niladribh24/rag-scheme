AVYAY - National Action Plan for Senior Citizens: Health and Shelter for Senior Citizens
Level: National
Category: Social Justice & Empowerment / Health & Shelter for Senior Citizens
Key Benefit: Annual recurring grants up to ₹42.11 Lakh for Senior Citizen Homes and ₹30.77 Lakh for Continuous Care Homes, non-recurring grants, plus 90% State Action Plan funding.

===================================================
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
===================================================

For Senior Citizens (Inmates/Beneficiaries)

* Must be a Senior Citizen.
* Must be indigent with no sufficient means of self-maintenance (as determined by State Govt), OR
* Must be afflicted with Alzheimer's disease / Dementia, or seriously ill requiring continuous nursing care (for Continuous Care Homes).

For Implementing Agencies (General & Voluntary Organizations)

* Eligible Entities: State/UT Governments, Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs), Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), Govt-recognized educational institutions, youth organizations (NYKS), Public Trusts, Section 25 (now Sec 8) Charitable Companies, Indian Red Cross Society.
* Registration & Experience (NGOs/VOs):
   * Registered under Societies Registration Act, 1860, or relevant State/UT Act for at least 2 years.
   * Minimum 2 years of social sector experience at the time of application.
   * Joint bank account in the name of the NGO/VO.
   * Continuous Care Homes: Credible track record in running Charitable Hospitals, Nursing Homes, Medical Institutions, or Colleges.
* Preference Order: Self Help Groups of Senior Citizens > CSR Foundations > Start-ups in elderly care > NGOs/Trusts.

===================================================
EXCLUSIONS
===================================================

* New Mobile Medicare Units are NOT supported under IPSrC.
* No Grant-in-Aid released for new Physiotherapy Clinics under SAPSrC.

===================================================
BENEFITS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
===================================================

Recurring Grants (Annual Grant-in-Aid per Unit)

* Senior Citizen Homes (25 Beneficiaries): ₹24,38,355 (Z Category) to ₹25,04,355 (X Category).
* Senior Citizen Homes (50 Beneficiaries / 50 Elderly Women): ₹41,12,228 (Z Category) to ₹42,11,228 (X Category).
* Continuous Care Homes (Min 20 Beneficiaries): ₹30,11,310 (Z Category) to ₹30,77,310 (X Category).

Non-Recurring Grants (Every 5 Years)

* Senior Citizen Homes (25 Indigent): ₹3,09,105 (includes CCTV & website development).
* Senior Citizen Homes (50 Indigent): ₹4,12,140.
* Continuous Care Homes (20 Indigent): ₹3,09,105.

Additional Financial Support & Conditions

* Funeral Expenses: Reimbursement up to ₹10,000 per deceased inmate or actual cost (whichever is lower).
* State Action Plan (SAPSrC): 90% project cost funding.
* Disbursal Mode: Released via E-anudaan portal and PFMS/EAT module in 2 equal half-yearly instalments (June 30 and Dec 31).
* Compliance Requirements: Attendance tracked via contactless IT system; live CCTV streaming on portal/website mandatory.

===================================================
APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS
===================================================

Application Process (Online via E-anudaan Portal)

* Step 01: Survey & Notification – State/UT surveys gap districts. If State cannot establish homes, Central Govt releases open notifications.
* Step 02: Proposal Submission – Online submission on E-anudaan portal (Fresh proposals by stipulated timeline; Renewal/Ongoing proposals with UCs before April 30th).
* Step 03: Verification & Screening – District Collector authenticates certificates and submits recommendations/inspection reports by June 15th. Central Screening Committee meets by June 20th; Secretary/Finance approval and sanction issued by June 25th.
* Step 04: Disbursal – 1st instalment released before June 30th; 2nd instalment released before December 31st based on attendance and inspection reports.

Documents Required

* Organizational/Legal: Registration Certificate, Memorandum of Association (MOA), Project License/Registration from District Authorities, Bye-Laws/Aims, Managing Committee list & details, PAN Card copy, Bond Execution under Rule 231(2) & 3.
* Financial & Annual Reports: Annual Reports (last 2 years for NGOs), Audited Statement of Accounts (Income-Expenditure, Balance Sheet, Audit Report), Utilization Certificate (GFR 12A / Rule 238 for ongoing projects), Itemized Budget Estimates, Bank Authorization Letter.
* Project Specific: Qualification-mapped Staff List, Beneficiary List, Notarized Rent/Lease Agreement (or Rural Area Certificate), Beneficiary Satisfactory Certificates (for renewals), State Action Plan & PFMS/EAT compliance documentation.
